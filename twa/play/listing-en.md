## Full description (≤ 4000)
```
AI Ustaz is a free AI tutor that helps you learn to read the Quran correctly. We start from the very beginning: letters, vowel marks and syllables — following the “Muallim Sani” method by Ahmadhadi Maksudi.

WHAT IS IN THE APP NOW
• Reading lessons. An introductory lesson and 17 lessons of the first stage: Arabic letters, vowel marks, syllables. Short exercises: find the letter, build the syllable, read aloud.
• Voice recording. Read aloud, record yourself and listen back — it is easier to notice how you are doing.
• Voice reading check (test mode). Tap “Check” and the AI suggests what to pay attention to: only gentle “Check yourself” hints, no grades and no “mistakes”. The recording is processed on our own server in Kazakhstan and deleted right after analysis.
• Daily prayer times calculated on your phone using the method of the Spiritual Administration of Muslims of Kazakhstan, for towns and villages of Kazakhstan. The next prayer and the time left until it.
• Qibla: the direction of the Kaaba — with the phone compass, or with a diagram and the sun.
• Quran: Surah al-Fatiha — text from the verified Tanzil database.
• An example of how AI Ustaz explains a reading mistake.
• Interface in Russian and Kazakh.

PLAIN AND HONEST
• Free. No ads. No purchases or subscriptions.
• No sign-up: your progress stays on your phone only.
• We do not sell or share your data.
• AI Ustaz is a study aid, not a replacement for a teacher. AI can be wrong; correct recitation is taught by an ustaz. The app does not grant ijazah and does not issue fatwas.
• Hanafi madhhab and Maturidi creed; recitation — Hafs from ‘Asim.
• Lessons and texts are drafts reviewed by an ustaz; the app keeps growing.

For users aged 16 and over.

AI Ustaz opens the website aiustaz.pro: new lessons and fixes arrive by themselves, without a store update.
```
